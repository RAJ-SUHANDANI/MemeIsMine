# Meme Is Mine

An independent, free browser meme studio by Raj Suhandani. Visitors browse image templates, an Indian image archive, and video/GIF templates; upload images; edit captions, stickers, effects and dimensions; export images or video; and save drafts/bookmarks on the current browser.

No account, database, cross-device sync or AI captioning exists. Reviews are local notes, not public testimonials. Remote template availability and browser video capabilities can vary. Do not invent usage counts, ratings, timing claims or community proof.
